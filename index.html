Your books. Your pace.
A little bit of luck.

readluck is built around a simple idea: you already have books you want to read. Sometimes you just need a nudge to choose one.

Draw your next read

Let chance make the call. Pick a book from your queue without scrolling through the same list again.

Keep your own library

Add books manually and organise them by status, format, genre or the lists you create for yourself.

See your reading story

Look back on books finished, watch your reading habits take shape, and celebrate progress without pressure.

A tiny ritual

From “what should I read?”
to “just one more chapter”.

No recommendations rabbit hole. No endless comparing. Just your books and a fresh way to choose.

01

Build your reading queue

Add the books you want to read. Keep track of what’s next, what you’ve started and what you’ve finished.

02

Make a lucky pick

Draw from your whole queue or narrow it down by a list, genre or format to fit your mood.

03

Keep the story going

Accept the pick, draw again if it’s not the moment, and keep a history of your draws. Your books stay yours.
